# Follow project-owned workflows
Use the pinned `project-workflows/<name>/SKILL.md` procedures before global fallback seeds, and `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` for organization-specific work. Preserve the authority ladder, write-safety rules and organization-specific QA guidance during updates. Keep conceptual/task pairs in active spikes, scratch non-authoritative, archives historical, and TODO as the coordination index. Keep agent policy proposals non-operative until explicitly adopted; human governance retains its separate owner.
